One model, one call: Search, recommendations, and fraud detection, based on what people do rather than what they say.
Large Behavioral Models go beyond traditional recommendation algorithms by learning from sequences of human actions to predict what people will do next. The exciting part is that one model can work across different products, platforms, and use cases.
This Sundai, we’ll have Rickard Brüel-Gabrielsson, MIT PhD and co-founder of Unbox AI, sharing his research in this field and introducing BehaviorGPT, Unbox AI’s Large Behavioral Model.
BehaviorGPT-v4 is trained on 150 billion actions across retail, engagement, and payments, including actions like searches, views, and purchases. You give it a short history of what someone did, and it predicts what they’re likely to do next. Read more: https://research.unboxai.com/behaviorgpt-v4-one-large-behavioral-model.pdf
What can behavioral models be used for?
> Shopping and personalization: Predict which products someone is likely to view or buy next based on their previous behavior.
> Fraud detection: Identify transactions that are unusual compared with a person's previous behavior.
> Digital experiences: Predict what content or interaction a person might engage with next.
> Business decisions: Use behavioral predictions to support decision-making.
You'll be able to access Unbox AI's tools to build and experiment with BehaviorGPT:
- SDK: Integrate BehaviorGPT into your projects.
- Portal: Access portal.unboxai.com to upload a catalog or import one from a store link.
- API keys: Connect your applications to the model.
- Ready-made widgets and a shopping assistant: Integrate them into your projects.
- MCP server: Connect BehaviorGPT with other tools and applications.
