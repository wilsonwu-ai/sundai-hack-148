## Why K4, and what changes
K4 is the only concept with no prior art for its core move (the open-prediction round), the strongest replay loop (V3 = 5), and a thought-provoking claim backed by a 2026 study (R3-F13/F14: people treat AI as an authority on their own behavior; predictions alone steer choices).

The skeptic's kill reason is real, so the design fixes it instead of hiding it:
1. **Four cards per turn, not the whole catalog.** Chance is 1/4 blind and 1/3 once you defy, printed next to every score. A preference model that learns your taste in Round 1 can call your defiance in Round 2, because people who defy still pick what they like among the rest.
2. **Honesty is tested, not promised.** A frozen simulation test proves a random player scores at chance (no rigging), and a consistent player gets called well above chance.
3. **Grafts:** sealed SHA-256 commitments shown before every tap (from K3); a non-sensitive behavioral fingerprint (taste, position habit, defiance style; the safe half of K2); one composite line on the share card, shown only once 20+ players exist (from K9); an optional engine duel in the Lab view, BehaviorGPT vs the local habit model, both sealed (K5's duel, made verifiable).

**Name:** DEFY. **Hook:** "Never let them predict your next move." (mirrors the 5.6M-view X meme, `research/x/x_signals.md`). **Card line:** "It told me what I'd pick. I picked something else. It saw that coming too."
